## Modifications
Source photographs were cropped and, where documented by their individual building source registers, perspective-rectified and mapped to reconstructed building/sign geometry. The reconstruction includes inferred dimensions and details. This evidence imports those existing final GLBs without editing their pixels or geometry, preserves original XY coordinates, offsets Z only by the established ground datum, applies review daylight with emission disabled in the temporary render scene, renders orthographic Cycles views, and adds building labels, leader lines and scope/license captions. These are derived 3D review views, not untouched photographs, exact photo matches, or a simultaneous January/November historical scene. No generative image editing was used.

Detailed crop coordinates, original-file hashes, source limitations and material mapping remain in the individual assets' source reference registers. Copyrighted view-only operator/property photographs used during model research are not incorporated into these images as texture pixels. QFRONT and Seibu are not included or altered.
