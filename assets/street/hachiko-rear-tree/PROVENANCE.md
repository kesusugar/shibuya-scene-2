# Evidence, originality and temporal scope

## What this candidate represents

One standalone H-PL-005 tree, rooted within the surviving Hachiko enclosure, informed by the November 1, 2025 branch view and the August 8, 2026 leafy view. These are two different views/times, not a photogrammetric scan or a calibrated reconstruction. The existing six core trees were checked by the preceding evidence task and did not match this enclosure location. They remain untouched.

The visible heavy trunk, low asymmetric forks, farther-reaching right-hand limbs, upright central leader, short cut stubs and horizontally marked brown/gray bark inform the model. The rear branches, small twigs, leaf arrangement, total crown extent and all metric dimensions are estimates. No botanical species is assigned. The whole crown is not captured in either source. Its complete outline cannot be authenticated. August presence is not proof of October 2026 persistence or unchanged condition.

## Bounded dimension estimate

The visible trunk is substantially narrower than an adult body height and about comparable to a large coping module, but those features are at different depths and none is a surveyed ruler. A 0.71 m base diameter and first heavy fork around 1.75 m above the inferred ground plane were selected as plausible review dimensions, within a roughly 0.55–0.8 m trunk / 1.5–2.2 m low-fork working envelope. This is contextual estimation, not measurement.

The extrapolated candidate is about 8.82 m tall, with about 8.88 × 7.03 m crown/branch footprint. The unseen crown was bounded to a plausible 8–10 m height / 8–10 m width / 6–8 m depth working envelope. Those are design bounds for a usable candidate, NOT confidence intervals or evidence that the real tree lies within them. Top/side branch ends leave the photographs. Hidden canopy depth, exact girth, heights, species, roots, position and yaw remain unresolved.

The local origin is the trunk's ground-contact centre. It is not the crown bounding-box centre. Parent-local placement is semantic only; every numeric world transform and yaw remains null. The integration owner must fit and confirm it, without assuming that existing code coordinates are measurements.
