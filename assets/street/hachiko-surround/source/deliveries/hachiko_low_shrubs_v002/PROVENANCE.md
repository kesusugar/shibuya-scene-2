## Visual reference

Personally inspected the user's broad Hachiko photo, the prior rear-tree component/app capture, and repository reference `july095` showing fine directional foliage sprays behind the statue. The repository's existing README attributes July094/095 to RuinDig / Yuki Uchida, 2025-07-13, CC BY 4.0. Those photographs are observation-only for this delivery; no photo pixels, photographs, or unverified download URLs are bundled. The exact tree species and current-world/October condition are not established.

The four RGBA needle-spray patterns are entirely procedural original artwork generated by `source/create_needle_atlas.py`; they are not extracted from the reference photograph. Geometry and new material source are authored for this project. No third-party stock texture/model has been added. Existing project source/assets retain their existing licensing; this package does not relicense them.

## Dimensions and uncertainty

All units are meters. The six original root positions are fixed exactly from the source formula and recorded terrain samples. Left roots are at local x = -2.63, -1.925, -1.22; right = 1.22, 1.925, 2.63; all local y = -1.45. Their root z is 0.9610693359375 (left) and 0.9055540466308594 (right), in the original Blender Z-up datum.

The source world XY reference is (11.568175521259887, -48.68573220011713), yaw 35°. This inherited placement is an estimate, not new surveying. Standard GLB Y-up conversion is handled by Blender's exporter; do not apply the yaw again.

New leaf-spray card dimensions range 0.14–0.19 m long and 0.13–0.18 m wide before curvature. Each contains 23 individually tapered fine leaves with estimated visible widths around 2–6 mm. These are visual modeling choices, not measurements from the photograph. The card carrier geometry is wider than the visible masked foliage.

The six-shrub combined world-aligned Blender Z-up carrier bounds are recorded in `validation/roundtrip.json`: approximately 5.115 × 3.807 × 1.176 m across both separated segments. This is not the size of a single shrub. Highest carrier point z ≈ 2.082 m. New crowns are slightly lower than the original spike-tipped crowns; roots, soil and enclosure positions are unchanged.

The image comparisons establish shape/appearance of this component under a controlled light, not location correctness, botanical fidelity, historical authenticity, app performance or photo-camera matching.
