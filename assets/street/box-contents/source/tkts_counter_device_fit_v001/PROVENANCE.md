# Provenance and scope

This package is an original-geometry local assembly study. It reuses only the authored counter upper shell from `shibu_hachi_tkts_2024_kit_v003` and the existing meshes from `shibu_hachi_tkts_tabletop_devices_v001`. Original inputs are bundled unchanged for reproducibility. No source asset is rebuilt, rescaled, rotated, retopologized, or repainted.

- JR EAST VIEW TOURISM AND SALES / and trip.: https://www.andtrip.jp/article/006193.html
- Published: 2024-09-19. The actual photograph date is unknown; presence in 2026 is unverified.

The local source image and close crops were visually inspected. The photograph shows a red work surface/edge extending from behind the tablet toward and beneath the closed laptop. It does not show a separate raised laptop plinth. Perspective and greater camera depth can account for the laptop's apparently higher image position. This does not prove metric coplanarity, plan dimensions, or final device positions.

Private source photographs and crops are not redistributed, embedded, copied into comparison images, or used as textures. The delivered comparison and support diagram contain only authored model geometry, computed contact polygons, and explanatory text. Source links do not confer a license to the original photograph, brand, or mark.

## Inferences retained

- The existing v003 coplanar red return is accepted as an explicitly estimated support candidate, not a measured installation.
- Device sizes, tablet tilt, stand folds, tabletop dimensions, and hidden closures are inherited estimates.
- Tablet and laptop translations are local assembly candidates; world coordinates and geographic/runtime placement are null.
- Laptop side overhang is a disclosed consequence of the existing estimated 304 mm device and 280 mm return. No enlargement or extra support is invented.
- Existing visible cable segments retain their exact geometry. A cable's silhouette meeting a device is not proof of an electrical connection or port. No new socket, lead, concealed route, or hardware is introduced.
- The former device-kit laptop underside at approximately +72 mm was an authoring datum. It was not a measured shelf height.

## Untouched scope

The original counter/sign/monitor/device packages remain unchanged. The two sign/monitor GLBs are retained as byte-identical reference inputs only and are not included in the counter/device output. Letter geometry and materials are not edited. No lower cabinet, floor, runtime, GitHub, QFRONT, Seibu Shibuya, walking display, FPS, merge, or deployment operation is included.
