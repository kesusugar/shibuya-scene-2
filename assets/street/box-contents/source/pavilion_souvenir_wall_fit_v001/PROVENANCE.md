## Why this section, rather than a full-room fit

The photographs support the left/right sequence and wood surfaces but not exact depth, absolute elevation, complete floor support, or window location. The main sample is shifted right so its counter-specific louver does not falsely span the souvenir fixture. One unchanged linked instance of its broad vertical panel extends only the observed upper wood backdrop, retaining the same lower sample cut. A full-height wall, floor, hidden support, or window would assert evidence not present.

All four root translations are provisional local-review estimates. Unit scale, source geometry, UVs, modifiers, materials, original procedural image payloads and the six-box topology are preserved. The new artifact is an arrangement, not a measurement, construction specification or new item count.
