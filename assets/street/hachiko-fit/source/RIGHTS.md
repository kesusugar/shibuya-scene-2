The completed fitting revision adds authored roof infill/closures, a four-sided tree bed, louvre support skin, four fitted beam bays with terminals, local side kerbs and the route connecting the existing guidance endpoint to A8. These additions use the same cited observations and existing scene geometry. Hidden surfaces, exact dimensions, equipment-face assignment and guidance routing are inferred; no new reference photograph or other-location kit geometry was imported.

New atlas pixels are generated by `make_fan_paving.py` from seeded numeric arrays. No source-photo pixels are read. Fan centres, sizes, hidden surfaces, fence section and post covers are estimates, not authenticated engineering details.
