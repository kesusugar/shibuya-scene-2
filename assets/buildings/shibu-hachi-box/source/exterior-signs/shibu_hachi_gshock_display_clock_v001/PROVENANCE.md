## What is independently authored

All 3D geometry, UVs, material setup, face raster drawing, segment shapes, front/back label meshes, scripts and rendered verification images were authored for this reconstruction. The face artwork is generated from polygon/line/text primitives using bundled open-license DejaVu fonts. No actual photograph or crop was used as an albedo, emission, bump, decal, backdrop or other texture. No source-image color sampling was used. Photographic reflections, glazing, advertisements and background artwork are not included.

The recreated brand words and visible historical face/back markings identify the photographed object. They are not a claim of ownership, affiliation, endorsement or a blanket grant to reuse G-SHOCK/CASIO marks. Neutral font contours approximate visible lettering, not an exact logo tracing. Any broader publication remains subject to the project's existing trademark/rights review.

## Excluded copyrighted references

The official photographs and the SHIBUYA BUNKA PROJECT photograph are copyrighted. No redistribution license was found. The package contains links and factual observations only, **not the photographs, crops or contact sheets containing them**. The `references/sources.json` manifest intentionally excludes local reference paths and image bytes.

## Estimates and omissions

The approximate head H/W ratio (~1.13), visible support/head ratio (~0.27), large black case, short lugs, inset red-bordered face, six-notch circular silver back and twin-foot support arrangement are source-supported. Metre dimensions, depths, radii, symmetric opposite-side details, actual materials, relief depths and concealed assembly are inferred.

The model includes major readable front labels, boxed rear 240, DW-5000, JAPAN B, ST.STEEL, 200M WATER RESISTANT, CASIO and Shock Resistant. Unresolved tiny face microtext is omitted explicitly. Static 2026 display content must remain date-labeled; it is not a claim about the running clock today. Advertising imagery and temporary exhibition graphics are excluded.
