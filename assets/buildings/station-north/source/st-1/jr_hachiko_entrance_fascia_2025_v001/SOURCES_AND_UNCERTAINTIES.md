## What is supported and what is not
Supported by J01/J02: rectangular fascia, green left field, approximate white JR mark with JR東日本 beneath it, dark 渋谷駅 on the right, red lower strip carrying 入口専用 and ENTRANCE above ONLY. Pale perimeter and shallow return are visible. The English line break is preserved.

Not measured: dimensions, world position/orientation, mounting height/depth, precise finish/colour, rim thickness, kerning, original font, official logo contours and concealed construction. All those are author estimates. The back is a minimal technical closure. A neutral review world is not geographic placement.

Not included: vertical Shibuya Station wording on the neighbouring pier; exit-only signage; no-entry symbols; ハチ公改札 / Hachiko Gate, line symbols or arrows on other interior signs; passage/portal structural bays. The target is not A8 or the western portal by the policebox.

Date boundary: early-2025 snapshot after relocation on 2025-01-26; not proof of state in October 2026. H-CV-001 policebox, full H-CV-002 entry, passage geometry and world placement remain unresolved.

## Independent content and rights
The new meshes, geometry/layout source and model-only renders were produced for this candidate. The text bodies reproduce short factual sign wording. The JR shape is a new simplified approximation, not a downloaded brand asset. JR identity/trademark rights are not granted by this delivery. The bundled font-derived subset is renamed FasciaSubset; original Noto copyright and complete SIL Open Font License are supplied. The embedded font is packed into the editable blend. No third-party photograph is redistributed.

Graphic surfaces have at most 1 mm of technical separation from the pale face to prevent coplanar rendering. This is not a measured construction detail or an assertion of raised letters.
