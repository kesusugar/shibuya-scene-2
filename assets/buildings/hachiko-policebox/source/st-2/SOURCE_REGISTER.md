## Modeling decisions and confidence

- Component existence and broad visual relationships are source-visible; exact coordinates remain estimated.
- Nominal scale was supplied as a first-pass modeling seed, not a measurement. No photogrammetry, surveyed radius or pixel-accuracy score is claimed.
- The outer surround has a short upper-left return and longer right return, not invented equal full-height legs.
- The upper-left support and the two lower support locations are independently positioned observed categories. P1 x240–266, y492–545 explicitly shows the upper segment/red strip of the lower-left support; P2 better exposes the lower-right support. The crowd-hidden lower-left continuation is isolated as an estimate. No full symmetric column system is inferred.
- Band curve, shell thickness, lining spacing, canopy projection, aperture mullion spacing and plain sign size are adjustable estimates.
- Lower doorway/backing/frame, masked support foot, threshold and one small hidden thickness transition are isolated in `ESTIMATED_CLOSURE`.
- The upper rear aperture is simplified to shallow panes and outline geometry; it contains no interior depth or room contents.
- Fine green surface lines are original geometric approximation. Surface construction/material remains unidentified.
- Full lateral/rear perimeter, exact station connection, all concealed lower supports, present-day alterations and ground burial are intentionally unresolved.

The supplied source generator and model-only renders contain original geometry and presentation, not redistributed third-party photo bytes.
